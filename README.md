## About

I am an Ingénieur Statisticien Économiste (ISE) working at the intersection of official statistics, econometrics, data science, and software engineering.

My background includes national accounts and industrial production indicators at Togo's national statistics office (INSEED), enterprise survey operations on a World Bank-funded project, and applied research in macroeconomics and finance. I supervised 15 field teams responsible for enterprise data collection and quality control.

I connect quantitative methods with product development, moving from raw data and econometric models to APIs, dashboards, and web applications that support better decisions.
