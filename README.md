## About

Economic Statistician Engineer (ISE) working at the intersection of official statistics, econometrics, and software development. My background covers national accounts and industrial production indices at Togo's national statistics office (INSEED), large-scale survey operations on a World Bank-funded project, and applied macro-financial research.

I build the full chain: from raw data to econometric models, and from models to APIs, dashboards, and web applications. Improved forecast precision by 20% on official economic indicators. Supervised 15 field teams on enterprise survey data quality. Co-author of a working paper on public debt and growth in Sub-Saharan Africa (MPRA #120429).

Currently building an interactive dashboard of Togolese economic indicators (R Shiny), a stock-market shock duration model (R), and web tools for SMEs and digital finance.
